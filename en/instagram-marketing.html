All questions
How often should you post on Instagram?

For most businesses two to four feed posts a week works well, plus stories. Consistency matters more than volume.

What's the best format for Instagram posts?

Portrait 4:5 (1080×1350) takes up the most space in the feed. Reels and stories are 9:16 (1080×1920). We deliver every visual in the right format.

Do you also reply to DMs?

Yes, in the Growth and Fully handled packages. Questions only you can answer are forwarded right away.
