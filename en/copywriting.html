Voice profile

Listen first, then write

Before we write a single post, we pin down how your business talks. That voice profile keeps every text consistent, whoever writes it.

  • Formal or informal?We choose one way of addressing people and stick to it.
  • Words you do and don't useTrade terms your customers search for, and jargon we avoid.
  • How much humour fits?From strictly professional to a wink.
  • What do you never say?Topics and claims that don't fit your business.

Questions

Questions about copywriting

Can't find your question? Call or email us and we'll explain.

All questions
How do you learn my tone of voice?

We start with a short interview and read your website, emails and earlier posts. From that we create a voice profile: the words you use, how formal you are and what you'd never say.

Do you also write for my website or newsletter?

Our focus is social media, but we also write the copy that goes with it, such as an About page, job ads or a short newsletter.

Do you use AI to write copy?

We use smart tools where they save time, but every text is written and checked by a person who knows your business. You approve everything before it goes live.

Which languages do you write in?

Dutch and English. Bilingual posts for LinkedIn are possible by arrangement.
