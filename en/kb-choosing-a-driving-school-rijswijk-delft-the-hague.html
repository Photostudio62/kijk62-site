Driving school in Rijswijk, Delft or The Hague? How to choose, and why Rijschool Fane stands out

A good website shows at a glance why you'd choose a business. We built one for Rijschool Fane. Here's what to look for when choosing driving lessons, and what Fane does differently.

By Arda GencClient & partner stories6 min readUpdated: November 2026

Short answer

Rijschool Fane is a driving school for Rijswijk, Delft, The Hague and the surrounding area. You get one-to-one lessons with a fixed instructor and a fixed lesson car, packages including the CBR exam and a 90-minute trial lesson for €55. Fane picks you up in 10 places, including Leidschendam, Voorburg, Ypenburg, Nootdorp, Wateringen, Den Hoorn and Delfgauw, and trains for manual, automatic, trailer (BE) and theory.

Five things to look for in a driving school

  1. A fixed instructor and lesson car. You build confidence faster and don't start from scratch every lesson.
  2. A trial lesson. You find out whether there's a click and roughly how many lessons you'll need.
  3. Clear packages. Check whether the CBR exam is included.
  4. Pick-up in your area. Saves travel time and you practise on the roads you'll actually drive.
  5. Accreditation and reviews. A driving school should be registered with the CBR. Reviews tell you how students experience it.

What Rijschool Fane does differently

  • A 90-minute trial lesson for €55: like a real lesson, so you know straight away where you stand.
  • One-to-one with a fixed instructor and a fixed lesson car.
  • Packages including the CBR exam.
  • Pick-up in 10 places, at over 100 locations: The Hague, Rijswijk, Delft, Voorburg, Leidschendam, Ypenburg, Nootdorp, Wateringen, Den Hoorn and Delfgauw.
  • Manual, automatic, trailer (BE) and theory, with motorcycle, scooter and taxi coming soon.
  • Registered with the CBR and a member of LBKR and VRB.

Every student who passes gets their own 'Passed!' post at Fane, like Afra above. Real students, the real lesson car and the trailer you practise with for your BE licence: visuals like that make a driving school credible, on the website and on social media.

See all courses, prices and the pick-up map on Rijschool Fane's website, or book a trial driving lesson in Rijswijk, Delft or The Hague straight away.

Behind the scenes: how we built fane.nl

Fane had a standard website with a blue header and three blocks. Nice, but it didn't say what and where. The new site starts with exactly that: 'Driving school in Rijswijk, Delft and The Hague', with the trial lesson and reviews right in view.

  • An interactive map showing where Fane picks you up.
  • Pages per region, so Fane is found for 'driving school Delft' and 'driving school Rijswijk'.
  • A knowledge base answering the questions students ask.
  • Bilingual, for international students in The Hague.
  • A playful mascot giving tips, just like our peeking eye.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a trial lesson at Rijschool Fane?

A trial lesson at Rijschool Fane lasts 90 minutes and costs €55 (according to fane.nl, November 2026).

Where does Rijschool Fane give lessons?

In and around Rijswijk, Delft and The Hague. Fane picks up in 10 places, including Voorburg, Leidschendam, Ypenburg, Nootdorp, Wateringen, Den Hoorn and Delfgauw.
