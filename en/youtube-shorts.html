Questions about YouTube Shorts

All questions
What's the difference between YouTube Shorts and TikTok?

Both are short vertical video. YouTube Shorts are also found through YouTube and Google search, so they often get discovered for longer.

Do I need a YouTube channel?

Yes. If you don't have one yet, we'll set it up with your logo, banner and a clear description.

How long can a Short be?

A Short is vertical and short. We keep most Shorts under a minute, because that's when viewers stay longest.
