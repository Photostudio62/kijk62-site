Working together

What does a social media marketing agency like KIJK62 do?

KIJK62 takes your social media off your hands completely. Together we set the goals, then we shoot photos and video in our own studio in Poeldijk, write the copy, schedule and post on every channel, reply to messages and send you a monthly report.

How much of my time does it take?

After the intro call and the studio day, mostly a few minutes a week: you approve the scheduled posts in one overview. We do the rest.

How soon can we start?

Usually within two to three weeks of the intro call. Your first content calendar is live in the first month.

Do I keep access to my accounts?

Yes. Your accounts stay in your company's name. We get admin rights and you always keep full access.

How do I approve posts?

You get an overview of all scheduled posts. For each post you click approve or leave a comment. You can do it on your phone.

Cost and results

What does outsourcing social media to KIJK62 cost?

You get a fixed monthly price, based on the number of channels, how often you want to post and how many studio days you need. We agree it after a free intro call.

What does outsourcing social media deliver?

Two things: time and customers. You get back the hours you now spend on social media yourself, and you build visibility with customers and candidates. Our ROI calculator shows how many new customers per month you need to earn back the investment.

How do you calculate the ROI of social media?

ROI is (return minus investment) divided by investment. The return consists of gross profit on new customers and the value of the time you get back. Our ROI calculator works it out for you.

How quickly will I see results?

A refreshed profile and regular posts stand out straight away. Reach and enquiries build up: allow a few months of steady rhythm before it really takes off.

What's in the monthly report?

Reach, engagement, follower growth, the best performing posts, enquiries via social media and what we'll do differently next month. In plain language, on one page.

How do I know if social media pays off?

You measure reach, engagement, website visits and enquiries via social media. You'll see it in the monthly report. To calculate in advance, use the ROI calculator or read what outsourcing social media costs.

Which channels do you post on?

Do you also help with the owner's own LinkedIn?

Websites

Do you also build websites?

Yes. KIJK62 builds professional custom websites, with design, copy, photos from our own studio, SEO and GEO. So your social media and your website are with one partner.

What does a website cost?

Every website is custom work, so there's no standard price. The price depends on the number of pages, features, languages and whether we create the copy and photos. After a free intro call you get a quote with a fixed price.

What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) means setting up your website so AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers understand and mention your business. We do this with clear answers, structured data and an llms.txt.

Is SEO included with a new website?

Yes, technical SEO is included as standard: speed, mobile, unique titles per page, schema.org, sitemap and local SEO.

Do you have an example of a website you built?

Yes, the new website of Rijschool Fane. See the before and after of fane.nl.

How do I get found in ChatGPT?

With GEO: short factual answers on your pages, structured data, FAQs and consistent business details. Read what GEO is and how it works.
