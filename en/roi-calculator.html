How we calculate

Transparent to the euro

No black box. These are the formulas behind the calculator.

CUSTOMERS

Gross profit on new customers

Extra customers per month × ramp-up factor × customer value × gross margin. The ramp-up factor is 0% in month 1, then 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% from month 5.

TIME

Value of your time

Hours per week × 52 ÷ 12 × your hourly value. You get that time back from the first month, because we take over the work.

BREAK-EVEN

Customers needed to break even

(Monthly investment − value of your time per month) ÷ (customer value × margin). This is the key question: is it achievable for your business?

ROI

Return in year 1

(Total return over 12 months − total investment) ÷ total investment × 100%.

Worked example

An installer in Naaldwijk

A fictional example to show the maths. A new customer brings in €2,500 in revenue on average, at a 40% margin. The owner now spends 4 hours a week on social media, and an hour of his time is worth €50.

  • At €1,250 a month: 0.4 customers needed(€1,250 − €867) ÷ €1,000. One new customer a month covers the investment comfortably.
  • At €2,000 a month: 1.2 customers needed(€2,000 − €867) ÷ €1,000.

All questions
How do you calculate the ROI of social media?

ROI is (return minus investment) divided by investment. For social media the return has two parts: gross profit on new customers (number of customers × customer value × margin) and the value of the time you get back (hours per week × 52 × your hourly value).

Why do you include a ramp-up period?

Social media doesn't work from day one. Building an audience takes time. So we count month 1 without new customers and ramp up to your full scenario over four months. That gives a more honest picture.

Is the outcome a guarantee?

No. The calculator uses your own assumptions. It shows what's needed to earn back the investment, so you can make an informed choice. In the intro call we check together whether your assumptions are realistic for your market.

What is the break-even point?

The number of new customers per month you need to earn back the monthly investment, after deducting the value of the time you get back. Everything above that is profit.

What does KIJK62 cost per month?

That depends on your channels, the number of posts and the studio days. After the intro call you get a fixed monthly price. The amount in the calculator is an example you can change yourself.
