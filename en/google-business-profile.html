All questions
What is a Google Business Profile?

The free business listing Google shows in Search and Maps, with your address, opening hours, photos, reviews and updates.

Does a Google Business Profile help SEO?

Yes, especially for local searches like 'installer Naaldwijk'. A complete, active profile with reviews makes the difference.

Can you remove reviews?

No, only Google can remove reviews that break its rules. We do help with a good reply and with asking happy customers for reviews.
