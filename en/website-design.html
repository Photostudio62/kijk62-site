    All questions
    What does a website from KIJK62 cost?

    Every website is custom work, so we don't quote a standard price up front. The price depends on the number of pages, features such as forms, a calculator or multiple languages, and whether we also create the copy and photos. After a free intro call you get a quote with a fixed price, so you know exactly where you stand.

    How long does it take to build a website?

    That depends on the size and on how quickly copy and visuals are ready. The quote includes a schedule with dates, from design to launch.

    What is GEO and why does it matter for my website?

    GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: setting up your website so AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers understand, trust and mention your business. We do this with clear, factual answers on every page, structured data (schema.org), an llms.txt file and a clear structure per topic.

    Do you include SEO with a new website?

    Yes, technical SEO is included as standard: fast loading, mobile-friendly, unique titles and descriptions per page, schema.org, a sitemap, logical internal links and local SEO for your region. So the foundations are right from day one.

    Do you also create the copy and photos for the website?

    Yes. That's the difference from most web agencies: we have our own photo studio in Poeldijk and write the copy ourselves, in your company's voice. You get a complete website, not just a design you have to fill yourself.

    Can you redesign my existing website?

    Yes. We first check what ranks well in Google, so you don't lose positions in the redesign. Old addresses are redirected to the new pages.

    Can the website be multilingual?

    Yes. Like this website, we can build your site in Dutch and English, with a language switch on every page and the right signals for Google.

    Do you also handle hosting, domain and maintenance?

    We advise you on domain and hosting and help with the move. Maintenance and small changes after launch can be arranged with us.
