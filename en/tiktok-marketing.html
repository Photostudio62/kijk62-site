All questions
Is TikTok right for my business?

If your audience or future colleagues are there, yes. Construction, installation, hospitality, beauty and retail often do well with real, short video.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Your team, product or workplace works too. If you do want to be on camera, we'll guide you.

How long is a good TikTok?

For most businesses 15 to 45 seconds works well. Holding the viewer matters more than length.
