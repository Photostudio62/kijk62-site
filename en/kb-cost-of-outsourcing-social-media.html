What does outsourcing social media cost? How the price is built up

The first question every business owner asks. An honest answer starts with: it depends. But on a few very concrete things.

By Arda GencStrategy & cost6 min readUpdated: November 2026

Short answer

The price of outsourcing social media depends on five things: the number of channels, how often you post, whether photos and video need to be created, whether the agency also replies to messages, and how much strategy and reporting you want. At KIJK62 you get a fixed monthly price after a free intro call. Whether it pays for itself, you work out with the ROI calculator.

  3. Fixed monthly price or hourly?
  4. FAQ

The five factors that set the price

  1. Number of channels. Each channel has its own formats, tone and audience. Two channels are less work than five.
  2. Posting frequency. Two posts a week per channel is a different package from five. Consistency matters more than volume.
  3. Creating content. Do you supply photos, or does the agency create them? A studio day or video takes time, but delivers weeks of posts.
  4. Replying and community. Answering messages and comments is daily work. Not every package includes it.
  5. Strategy and reporting. A monthly report with advice and a quarterly review give direction, but also take time.

What you get back

Outsourcing social media delivers two things. First, time: the hours you now spend on posts in the evening. Second, visibility: a steady rhythm builds trust with customers and candidates.

TIP FROM ARDA

Count your own time. Four hours a week at €50 an hour is already over €850 a month of time you get back.
