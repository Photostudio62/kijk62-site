All questions
What's the difference between a LinkedIn company page and a personal profile?

A company page is your organisation's official face: services, vacancies and news. Your personal profile is your own network. Posts from people often reach more people on LinkedIn than posts from company pages, so we work on both.

Do you write posts on my behalf?

Yes, by arrangement. We interview you briefly, learn how you talk and write posts in your voice. You approve everything before it goes live.

What makes a good LinkedIn profile?

A professional profile photo, a banner that shows what you do, a headline with your trade and who you work for, an About section in plain language, and featured items such as cases, services or vacancies.

Does LinkedIn help with hiring?

Yes. Candidates look at your company page and your team's profiles. An active page with real photos of your people makes you a more attractive employer.

How quickly will I see results on LinkedIn?

A refreshed profile stands out straight away. Reach and enquiries build up through regular posting. Allow a few months of steady rhythm before it really takes off.
