All questions
What does social media management include?

At KIJK62, everything: strategy and content plan, photography and video, copywriting, scheduling and posting, replying to messages and comments, keeping your profiles up to date and a monthly report with advice.

How often do you post?

We agree that in the content plan. Most businesses choose two to five posts per week per channel. The content planner on the packages page shows what that means per month.

Do I keep access to my accounts?

Yes. Your accounts stay in your company's name. We get admin rights and you always keep full access.

Do you also reply to messages?

Yes, in the Growth and Fully handled packages. Questions only you can answer, such as a quote or a complaint, are forwarded to you straight away.

What's in the monthly report?

Reach, engagement, follower growth, the best performing posts, enquiries via social media and what we'll do differently next month. In plain language, on one page.
