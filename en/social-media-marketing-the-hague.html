All questions
Does KIJK62 work for businesses in The Hague?

Yes. Our studio in Poeldijk is right next to The Hague. We work for businesses in the city centre, Scheveningen, Loosduinen, Laak and Ypenburg, among others, and in Rijswijk, Delft, Leidschendam-Voorburg and Zoetermeer.

How far is the studio from The Hague?

The studio in Poeldijk is on the south-west side of The Hague. From Loosduinen it's about ten minutes by car, from the city centre about twenty.

Do you also shoot on location in The Hague?

Yes. We shoot photos and video at your office, shop or restaurant.

Do you work with international businesses in The Hague?

Yes. The Hague is home to many international companies and organisations. We create content and write copy in both Dutch and English.
