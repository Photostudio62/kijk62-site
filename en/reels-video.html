All questions
How long is a good reel?

Most of the reels we make are 15 to 45 seconds. The first three seconds matter more than the length: that's where viewers decide whether to stay.

Where do you film?

In our studio in Poeldijk, or on location at your business. Often during the same studio day as the photos.

Do I have to be on camera?

Not necessarily, but it often works well. We guide you so it stays natural. You can also show your team, your product or your workplace.

Do you add subtitles?

Yes, always. Many people watch without sound, so every video gets subtitles in your brand style.

Which channels do you publish video on?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and LinkedIn. Each channel gets the right format and a fitting caption.
