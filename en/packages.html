In short

KIJK62 offers three social media packages: Visible (1–2 channels, quarterly studio shoot), Growth (3–4 channels, with video and replying) and Fully handled (all channels, monthly studio day, owner's LinkedIn profile and Google Business Profile). Each package has a fixed monthly price.

All questions
What does outsourcing social media to KIJK62 cost?

You get a fixed monthly price, based on the number of channels, how often you want to post and how many studio days you need. We agree it after a free intro call, with no surprises afterwards.

Can I switch packages later?

Yes. Start with Visible and want more? We scale up. Every quarter we check together whether your package still fits.

Can I buy a single service?

Yes. For example a LinkedIn refresh, a studio day for team portraits or a set of reels. Ask about it in the intro call.

What's the difference between Growth and Fully handled?

With Fully handled we also take care of your personal LinkedIn profile and Google Business Profile, plan a studio day or location shoot every month and review the results with you monthly.
