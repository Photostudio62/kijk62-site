All questions
How soon can we start?

Usually within two to three weeks of the intro call. Your first content calendar is live in the first month.

What do I need to get started?

Access to your social media accounts (or we create them together), your logo and brand style if you have them, and an hour for the intro call. We arrange the rest.

How do I approve posts?

You get an overview of all scheduled posts. For each post you click approve or leave a comment. You can do it on your phone.

How often are we in touch?

You have one fixed point of contact. Every month you get a report, and with Fully handled we also review the results together.
