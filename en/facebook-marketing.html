Is Facebook still worthwhile for businesses?

Often, for local businesses. Many customers in the area use Facebook to find businesses, read reviews and ask questions.

Do you post the same content on Instagram and Facebook?

Sometimes, but we adapt the copy and format per channel. On Facebook a slightly longer, local caption often works better.

Do you also manage Facebook groups?

By arrangement. We can prepare posts for relevant local groups, for you or us to publish, following each group's rules.
