Which social media channels suit your business?

You don't have to be everywhere. You have to be where your customers are, and do it well.

By Arda GencChannels5 min readUpdated: November 2026

Short answer

Choose by audience and goal. Business clients and staff: LinkedIn. Local consumers: Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile. Younger audiences and recruiting tradespeople: TikTok and Instagram Reels. Explainers that should be found for a long time: YouTube Shorts. Start with two channels and do them well.

Frequently asked questions

Should my business be on TikTok?

Only if your audience or future colleagues are there. It often works well for construction, installation, hospitality, beauty and retail.
