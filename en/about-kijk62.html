About KIJK62

Our job is looking.

'Kijk' is Dutch for 'look'. We look at your business the way your customer sees it, at what works on social media and what doesn't, and through the lens of our own camera. That's where the name comes from, and the eye in our logo.

In short

KIJK62 is a social media marketing agency in Poeldijk (Westland), near The Hague, with its own photo studio. The agency helps SMEs in Westland, The Hague and across the Netherlands grow their online visibility by taking social media completely off their hands.

All questions
Where is KIJK62 based?

KIJK62 is based in Poeldijk, in the municipality of Westland, at ABC Westland 259, right next to The Hague. Our own photo studio is there too. Visits by appointment only.

Why is it called KIJK62?

'Kijk' is Dutch for 'look'. Looking is our job: at your business the way your customer sees it, and at what works on social media. The eye in our logo stands for that.

What kind of businesses does KIJK62 work for?

SMEs and business owners who want to be more visible online: from installers and growers to consultants, restaurants, salons and healthcare practices. In Dutch and English.
